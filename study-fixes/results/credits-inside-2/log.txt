=== K07 | is there a photography concentration n what classes do u take
--- by=one_call status=answered sec=10.75 err=None read: needs=[] limit=none topic=program asked=read q='Is there a photography concentration, and what classes do you take?' general=None
--- tools=['embedding', 'read_message', 'read_message', 'read_message', 'embedding', 'pick_catalog', 'pick_units', 'pick_units', 'pick_units', 'write_answer'] path={'topic': 'program', 'by': 'read'}
--- lookups=[]
Yes. Photography Concentration is within the Visual Arts BA (Bachelor of Arts). [1,22]

Required courses: choose 1 of ARTS 101 Fundamentals of Drawing or ARTS 201 Basic Painting; ARTS 202 Basic Sculpture; ARTS 207 Digital Photography; ARTS 211 Basic Art & Technology; ARHT 214 Photography Concepts and Histories; and ARTS 206 Black and White Photography. [1]

Also take one course from the listed Arts Upper-level Elective options, including ARTS 302 Intermediate Drawing, ARTS 303 Intermediate Painting, ARTS 305 Digital Drawing & Painting, ARTS 307 Intermediate Sculpture, ARTS 308 Bronze Casting and Welding, ARTS 311 Intermediate Ceramics, ARTS 320 Video Art, ARTS 323 Physical Computing, ARTS 325 Intermediate 3D Animation, ARTS 327 Installation Art, ARTS 330 The Artist's Book, ARTS 331 Art as Therapy, ARTS 333 Digital Fabrication, ARTS 335 Business of Visual Arts, ARTS 390 Topics, ARHT 340 Performance and Art, or CNTP 315 Art on the Internet. [1]

Choose at least 3 Photography Electives: ARTS 312 Intermediate Film Photography, ARTS 315 Medium and Large Format Photography, ARTS 318 Intermediate Digital Photography, ARTS 324 Documentary Photography, ARTS 328 Portrait Photography, or ARTS 406 Advanced Photography. [1]

Also required: CNTP 388 Co-op/Internship Contemporary Arts and ARTS 408 Senior Thesis Exhibition. [1]

Course details: ARTS 206 and ARTS 207 are 4 credits each and have no published prerequisites; ARTS 206 carries Gen Ed 18-Culture & Creativity (GECC). [4,5] ARTS 312 and ARTS 315 are 4 credits each; each requires ARTS 206. [6,7] ARTS 318 is 4 credits and requires ARTS 207. [8] ARTS 324 is 4 credits and requires either ARTS 206 or ARTS 207. [9]

Sources: [1](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/programs/AH-BA-ARTS) [4](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS207) [5](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS206) [6](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS312) [7](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS315) [8](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS318) [9](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS324) [22](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/programs)
