=== K07 | is there a photography concentration n what classes do u take
--- by=one_call status=answered sec=15.66 err=None read: needs=[] limit=none topic=program asked=read q='Is there a photography concentration, and what classes do you take?' general=None
--- tools=['embedding', 'read_message', 'read_message', 'read_message', 'embedding', 'pick_catalog', 'pick_units', 'pick_units', 'pick_units', 'write_answer'] path={'topic': 'program', 'by': 'read'}

Yes. Photography is a concentration in the Visual Arts BA (Bachelor of Arts). The Visual Arts Minor is also published. [19][1]

The concentration requires ARHT 214 PHOTOGRAPHY CONCEPTS AND HISTORIES and ARTS 206 BLACK AND WHITE PHOTOGRAPHY; ARTS 202 BASIC SCULPTURE and ARTS 211 BASIC ART & TECHNOLOGY; and one of ARTS 101 FUNDAMENTALS OF DRAWING or ARTS 201 BASIC PAINTING. [1]

It also requires one upper-level arts elective from the listed choices; three photography electives from ARTS 312 INTERMEDIATE FILM PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 315 MEDIUM AND LARGE FORMAT PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 318 INTERMEDIATE DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 324 DOCUMENTARY PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 328 PORTRAIT PHOTOGRAPHY, and ARTS 406 ADVANCED PHOTOGRAPHY; CNTP 388 CO-OP/INTERNSHIP CONTEMPORARY ARTS; and ARTS 408 SENIOR THESIS EXHIBITION. The page notes: “Complete 1 Additional Required Course from the following list: - 300-level Art History (ARHT) Course” and lists ARHT 204 INTRODUCTION TO GLOBAL ART TRADITIONS and ARHT 245 MASTERPIECES IN WESTERN CIVILIZATION. [1]

ARTS 206 is 4 credits, has no published prerequisites, and carries Gen Ed 18-Culture & Creativity (GECC). [3] ARTS 312 and ARTS 315 are each 4 credits; each requires ARTS 206. [4][6] ARTS 318 is 4 credits and requires ARTS 207 DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY. [5] ARTS 406 is 4 credits and requires one of ARTS 312, ARTS 315, ARTS 318, ARTS 324, or ARTS 328. [7]

Sources: [1](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/programs/AH-BA-ARTS) [3](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS206) [4](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS312) [5](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS318) [6](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS315) [7](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS406) [19](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/programs)
