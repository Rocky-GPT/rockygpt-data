=== 15-g8 | tips for not being nervous during presentations?
--- by=general status=answered sec=4.08 err=None read: needs=[] limit=none topic=how_to asked=own_words q='' general='Tips for not being nervous during presentations?'
**General answer** (from general knowledge, not Ramapo's pages):
- Practice aloud a few times, ideally with one person listening, so the opening feels familiar.
- Take a slow breath before you begin, and pause between points; you don’t need to rush.
- Use brief notes with key ideas rather than memorizing every word.
- Look at a few friendly faces or just above the audience, and remember they’re there to hear your ideas—not judge every stumble.
- If you lose your place, pause, check your notes, and continue.

=== 18-g8 | whats a good way to memorize spanish vocab
--- by=general status=answered sec=3.36 err=None read: needs=[] limit=off_topic topic=how_to asked=own_words q='' general='What is a good way to memorize Spanish vocabulary?'
**General answer** (from general knowledge, not Ramapo's pages): Use spaced repetition: review a small set of words today, then again tomorrow, in a few days, and a week later. Make flashcards with the Spanish word on one side and a short phrase or image on the other, and test yourself both ways. Practice using each new word in your own simple sentence so you remember how it works in context.
