=== 02-s1 | kinda scared to ask but how do i drop a class
--- by=one_call status=answered sec=6.59 err=None read: needs=[] limit=none topic=how_to asked=own_words q='How do I drop a class?' general=None
Sorry to hear that!

No need to be scared—students can drop a course. If you’re deciding, it’s strongly recommended that you seek advice from your instructor, academic advisor, and/or relevant professional staff; dropping may affect financial aid, progress toward graduation, housing, athletics, or campus activities. [1]

- During the add/drop/100% refund period, drop online in Web Self Service. The course won’t appear on your transcript. [1][17]
- During the 50% refund period, drop in Web Self Service or submit a 50% Drop Form to the Registrar. [17]
- After the refund period, you may withdraw through Web Self Service or use a withdrawal form. A W will appear on your transcript, there is no refund, and you cannot withdraw after the published deadline. Check the Academic Calendar for deadlines: [https://www.ramapo.edu/academic-calendars/](https://www.ramapo.edu/academic-calendars/) [1][20]

The Registrar can help with the process. Dropping below full-time may adjust your aid; contact Financial Aid if you’re concerned about that. [3]

- **Office of the Provost**: Phone: (201) 684-7330. Email: provost@ramapo.edu. Location: first floor of the Birch Mansion. [31]
- **Registrar**: Phone: (201) 684-7695. Email: reg@ramapo.edu. Office: D-224. [31]

Sources: [1](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/quicklinks/academicpolicies#dropping-or-withdrawing-from-a-course1) [3](https://www.ramapo.edu/finaid/faqs/) [17](https://www.ramapo.edu/registrar/registrationinfo/) [20](https://www.ramapo.edu/registrar/withdrawing-leave-of-absence/) [31](https://www.ramapo.edu/campus-directory/)
