=== K07 | is there a photography concentration n what classes do u take
--- by=one_call status=answered sec=10.15 err=None read: needs=[] limit=none topic=program asked=read q='Is there a photography concentration, and what classes do you take?' general=None
--- tools=['embedding', 'read_message', 'read_message', 'read_message', 'embedding', 'pick_catalog', 'pick_units', 'pick_units', 'pick_units', 'write_answer'] path={'topic': 'program', 'by': 'read'}
--- lookups=[]
Yes. The Visual Arts BA (Bachelor of Arts) offers a Photography Concentration. [1]

You take ARTS 202 BASIC SCULPTURE, ARTS 207 DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY, and ARTS 211 BASIC ART & TECHNOLOGY; one of ARTS 101 FUNDAMENTALS OF DRAWING or ARTS 201 BASIC PAINTING; ARHT 204 INTRODUCTION TO GLOBAL ART TRADITIONS and ARHT 245 MASTERPIECES IN WESTERN CIVILIZATION, plus one additional 300-level Art History (ARHT) course; one listed Arts Upper-level Elective; and at least three Photography Electives. [1]

Photography Lower-level Required Courses: ARHT 214 PHOTOGRAPHY CONCEPTS AND HISTORIES and ARTS 206 BLACK AND WHITE PHOTOGRAPHY. [1]

Photography Electives: choose at least three of ARTS 312 INTERMEDIATE FILM PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 315 MEDIUM AND LARGE FORMAT PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 318 INTERMEDIATE DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 324 DOCUMENTARY PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 328 PORTRAIT PHOTOGRAPHY, and ARTS 406 ADVANCED PHOTOGRAPHY. [1]

You also take CNTP 388 CO-OP/INTERNSHIP CONTEMPORARY ARTS and ARTS 408 SENIOR THESIS EXHIBITION. [1]

ARTS 206 BLACK AND WHITE PHOTOGRAPHY is 4 credits, has no published prerequisites, and carries Gen Ed 18-Culture & Creativity (GECC). [4] ARTS 207 DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY is 4 credits and has no published prerequisites. [3] ARTS 312 INTERMEDIATE FILM PHOTOGRAPHY is 4 credits; its prerequisite is ARTS 206 BLACK AND WHITE PHOTOGRAPHY. [5] ARTS 315 MEDIUM AND LARGE FORMAT PHOTOGRAPHY is 4 credits; its prerequisite is ARTS 206 BLACK AND WHITE PHOTOGRAPHY. [7] ARTS 318 INTERMEDIATE DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY is 4 credits; its prerequisite is ARTS 207 DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY. [6] ARTS 324 DOCUMENTARY PHOTOGRAPHY is 4 credits; its prerequisite is one of ARTS 206 BLACK AND WHITE PHOTOGRAPHY or ARTS 207 DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY. [8]

Sources: [1](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/programs/AH-BA-ARTS) [3](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS207) [4](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS206) [5](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS312) [6](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS318) [7](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS315) [8](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS324)
