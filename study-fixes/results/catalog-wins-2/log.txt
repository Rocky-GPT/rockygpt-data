=== K07 | is there a photography concentration n what classes do u take
--- by=one_call status=answered sec=10.49 err=None read: needs=[] limit=none topic=program asked=read q='Is there a photography concentration, and what classes do you take?' general=None
--- tools=['embedding', 'read_message', 'read_message', 'read_message', 'embedding', 'pick_catalog', 'pick_units', 'pick_units', 'pick_units', 'write_answer'] path={'topic': 'program', 'by': 'read'}

Yes. The catalog lists a Photography Concentration in the Visual Arts BA (Bachelor of Arts). [1, 19]

Required courses: ARTS 202 BASIC SCULPTURE, ARTS 207 DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 211 BASIC ART & TECHNOLOGY; ARHT 214 PHOTOGRAPHY CONCEPTS AND HISTORIES and ARTS 206 BLACK AND WHITE PHOTOGRAPHY; CNTP 388 CO-OP/INTERNSHIP CONTEMPORARY ARTS; and ARTS 408 SENIOR THESIS EXHIBITION. Choose 1 of ARTS 101 FUNDAMENTALS OF DRAWING or ARTS 201 BASIC PAINTING; complete the Art History requirement (the catalog note says complete 1 additional 300-level Art History (ARHT) course, with ARHT 204 INTRODUCTION TO GLOBAL ART TRADITIONS and ARHT 245 MASTERPIECES IN WESTERN CIVILIZATION listed); choose 1 Arts Upper-level Elective; and choose 3 of ARTS 312 INTERMEDIATE FILM PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 315 MEDIUM AND LARGE FORMAT PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 318 INTERMEDIATE DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 324 DOCUMENTARY PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 328 PORTRAIT PHOTOGRAPHY, or ARTS 406 ADVANCED PHOTOGRAPHY. [1]

Relevant photography courses are 4 credits each: ARTS 206 (no published prerequisites; Gen Ed 18-Culture & Creativity (GECC)), ARTS 207 (no published prerequisites), ARTS 312 and ARTS 315 (each requires ARTS 206), ARTS 318 (requires ARTS 207), and ARTS 406 (requires one of ARTS 312, ARTS 315, ARTS 318, ARTS 324, or ARTS 328). [2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7]

Sources: [1](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/programs/AH-BA-ARTS) [2](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS207) [3](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS318) [4](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS406) [5](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS312) [6](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS206) [7](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS315) [19](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/programs)
