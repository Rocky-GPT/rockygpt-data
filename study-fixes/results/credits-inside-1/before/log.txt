=== K07 | is there a photography concentration n what classes do u take
--- by=one_call status=answered sec=17.8 err=None read: needs=[] limit=none topic=program asked=read q='Is there a photography concentration and what classes do you take?' general=None
--- tools=['embedding', 'read_message', 'read_message', 'read_message', 'embedding', 'pick_catalog', 'pick_units', 'pick_units', 'pick_units', 'write_answer', 'embedding', 'write_answer'] path={'topic': 'program', 'by': 'read'}
--- lookups=[]
Yes. Photography is a concentration in the Visual Arts BA (Bachelor of Arts). [1] It requires: [1]
- Choose 1 lower-level course: ARTS 101 FUNDAMENTALS OF DRAWING or ARTS 201 BASIC PAINTING.
- ARTS 202 BASIC SCULPTURE, ARTS 207 DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY, and ARTS 211 BASIC ART & TECHNOLOGY.
- Art history: ARHT 204 INTRODUCTION TO GLOBAL ART TRADITIONS or ARHT 245 MASTERPIECES IN WESTERN CIVILIZATION; complete 1 additional required course from a 300-level Art History (ARHT) Course.
- Choose 1 arts upper-level elective from the list in the Visual Arts BA requirements. [1]
- ARHT 214 PHOTOGRAPHY CONCEPTS AND HISTORIES and ARTS 206 BLACK AND WHITE PHOTOGRAPHY.
- Choose 3 or more photography electives: ARTS 312 INTERMEDIATE FILM PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 315 MEDIUM AND LARGE FORMAT PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 318 INTERMEDIATE DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 324 DOCUMENTARY PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 328 PORTRAIT PHOTOGRAPHY, or ARTS 406 ADVANCED PHOTOGRAPHY.
- CNTP 388 CO-OP/INTERNSHIP CONTEMPORARY ARTS and ARTS 408 SENIOR THESIS EXHIBITION. [1]

Among those electives, ARTS 312 and 315 each require ARTS 206; ARTS 318 requires ARTS 207. ARTS 406 requires one of ARTS 312, 315, 318, 324, or 328. [8][9][7][10] ARTS 206, 207, 312, 315, 318, and 406 are each 4 credits. ARTS 206 has the Gen Ed attribute Gen Ed 18-Culture & Creativity (GECC); ARTS 206 and 207 have no published prerequisites. [6][5][8][9][7][10]

Sources: [1](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/programs/AH-BA-ARTS) [5](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS207) [6](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS206) [7](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS318) [8](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS312) [9](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS315) [10](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS406)
