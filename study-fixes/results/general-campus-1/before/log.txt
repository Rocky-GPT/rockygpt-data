**General answer** (from general knowledge, not Ramapo's pages): Renting is usually cheaper if you only need the book for one course and don’t need to keep it. Buying can be better if you’ll reuse it, want to resell it, or need to write in it; compare the total rental cost and return rules with the used-book price.

Ramapo says students may choose to rent or buy books new or used; it doesn’t say which is better overall. Its Bookstore says renting can save up to 80% off the price of a new book. [15]

To find your required books, log into [web self-service](https://experience.elluciancloud.com/rconj/?name=homepage&accessibility=true), click “Student Services and Financial Aid,” select Registration, then “Buy Books Now from efollett.com.” This generates a list based on your current course registration status. You can pick up books at the bookstore or have them shipped to your home. [4]

The Bookstore also recommends ordering as early as possible to get USED copies and save money. [15]
