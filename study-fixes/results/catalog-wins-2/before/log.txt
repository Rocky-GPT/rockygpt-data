=== K07 | is there a photography concentration n what classes do u take
--- by=one_call status=answered sec=10.1 err=None read: needs=[] limit=none topic=program asked=read q='Is there a photography concentration, and what classes do you take?' general=None
--- tools=['embedding', 'read_message', 'read_message', 'read_message', 'embedding', 'pick_catalog', 'pick_units', 'pick_units', 'pick_units', 'write_answer'] path={'topic': 'program', 'by': 'read'}
--- lookups=[]
Yes. Photography Concentration is in the Visual Arts BA (Bachelor of Arts). [20] Requirements: choose at least 1 of ARTS 101 FUNDAMENTALS OF DRAWING or ARTS 201 BASIC PAINTING; take ARTS 202 BASIC SCULPTURE, ARTS 207 DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY, and ARTS 211 BASIC ART & TECHNOLOGY; complete ARHT 204 INTRODUCTION TO GLOBAL ART TRADITIONS and ARHT 245 MASTERPIECES IN WESTERN CIVILIZATION, plus 1 additional required 300-level Art History (ARHT) Course; choose 1 Arts Upper-level Elective from the listed options; take ARHT 214 PHOTOGRAPHY CONCEPTS AND HISTORIES and ARTS 206 BLACK AND WHITE PHOTOGRAPHY; choose at least 3 Photography Electives: ARTS 312 INTERMEDIATE FILM PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 315 MEDIUM AND LARGE FORMAT PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 318 INTERMEDIATE DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 324 DOCUMENTARY PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 328 PORTRAIT PHOTOGRAPHY, or ARTS 406 ADVANCED PHOTOGRAPHY; take CNTP 388 CO-OP/INTERNSHIP CONTEMPORARY ARTS and ARTS 408 SENIOR THESIS EXHIBITION. [1]
ARTS 207 DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY is 4 credits; no published prerequisites. [3] ARTS 206 BLACK AND WHITE PHOTOGRAPHY is 4 credits, has no published prerequisites, and carries Gen Ed 18-Culture & Creativity (GECC). [4] ARTS 312 INTERMEDIATE FILM PHOTOGRAPHY is 4 credits; Prerequisite Prerequisite Course complete all of: - ARTS 206 BLACK AND WHITE PHOTOGRAPHY. [5] ARTS 315 MEDIUM AND LARGE FORMAT PHOTOGRAPHY is 4 credits; Prerequisite Prerequisite Course complete all of: - ARTS 206 BLACK AND WHITE PHOTOGRAPHY. [7] ARTS 318 INTERMEDIATE DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY is 4 credits; Prerequisite Prerequisite Course complete all of: - ARTS 207 DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY. [6] ARTS 324 DOCUMENTARY PHOTOGRAPHY is 4 credits; Prerequisite Select 1 Course complete one of: - ARTS 206 BLACK AND WHITE PHOTOGRAPHY - ARTS 207 DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY. [8]

Sources: [1](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/programs/AH-BA-ARTS) [3](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS207) [4](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS206) [5](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS312) [6](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS318) [7](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS315) [8](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS324) [20](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/programs)
