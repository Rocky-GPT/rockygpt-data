Ramapo’s catalog does not list Criminology as a major. It does list a Sociology BA with a Criminology concentration: required SOCI 245 CRIMINOLOGY and 2 electives chosen from SOCI 248 CRIME AND MEDIA, SOCI 255 HUMAN RIGHTS IN THE CONTEXT OF LAW ENFORCEMENT, SOCI 315 SOCIOLOGY OF DEVIANCE, SOCI 345 RACE, CRIME AND JUSTICE, or SOCI 390 TOPICS. === K07 | is there a photography concentration n what classes do u take
--- by=one_call status=answered sec=10.76 err=None read: needs=[] limit=none topic=program asked=read q='Is there a photography concentration, and what classes do you take?' general=None
--- tools=['embedding', 'read_message', 'read_message', 'read_message', 'embedding', 'pick_catalog', 'pick_units', 'pick_units', 'pick_units', 'write_answer'] path={'topic': 'program', 'by': 'read'}
--- lookups=[]
Yes. The catalog lists a Photography Concentration in the Visual Arts BA (Bachelor of Arts). [1][20]

You must take ARHT 214 PHOTOGRAPHY CONCEPTS AND HISTORIES and ARTS 206 BLACK AND WHITE PHOTOGRAPHY. [1] Also take one lower-level course: ARTS 101 FUNDAMENTALS OF DRAWING or ARTS 201 BASIC PAINTING; one Arts upper-level elective from the listed options; and at least three photography electives: ARTS 312 INTERMEDIATE FILM PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 315 MEDIUM AND LARGE FORMAT PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 318 INTERMEDIATE DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 324 DOCUMENTARY PHOTOGRAPHY, ARTS 328 PORTRAIT PHOTOGRAPHY, or ARTS 406 ADVANCED PHOTOGRAPHY. [1] Complete CNTP 388 CO-OP/INTERNSHIP CONTEMPORARY ARTS and ARTS 408 SENIOR THESIS EXHIBITION. [1]

ARTS 206 is 4 credits, has no published prerequisites, and carries Gen Ed 18-Culture & Creativity (GECC). [5] ARTS 312 and ARTS 315 are each 4 credits; both require ARTS 206. [6][7] ARTS 406 is 4 credits and requires one of ARTS 312, ARTS 315, ARTS 318, ARTS 324, or ARTS 328. [8]

Sources: [1](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/programs/AH-BA-ARTS) [5](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS206) [6](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS312) [7](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS315) [8](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/courses/ARTS406) [20](https://catalog.ramapo.edu/programs)
